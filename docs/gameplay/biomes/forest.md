# Biome: Forest

**Altitude Range:** 3000m - 5000m
**World Partition Layer:** `DL_PCG_Biome_Forest`

## Overview
The Cloud Forest is the lungs of the Mistspire. Between 3000 and 5000 meters, the moisture from the lower mists meets the warmer air of the mid-heights, creating a lush, vertical jungle that clings to every available surface. It is a riot of green and silver.

## Visual Description
Towering spires are swallowed by thick, bioluminescent moss and massive, gravity-defying trees. Giant "Mist-Whales" (large, floating plant-life) drift between the peaks, their long, trailing roots providing makeshift bridges for those brave enough to climb them. The air is thick with humidity and the sweet, heavy scent of blooming spores.

## Lore Fragment: The Green Breath
> "The forest doesn't just grow on the spires; it consumes them. I found an old outpost today—stone walls three feet thick, completely split apart by a single vine. The greenery here has a hunger I’ve never seen elsewhere. It’s as if the plants are trying to reach the sun before the spires do."
> — *Silas, Botanist of the Heights*

## Flora & Fauna
- **Spore-Lamps:** Large, bulbous fungi that emit a soft, pulsing green light. They release clouds of buoyant spores that can be used to gain extra lift during a glide.
- **Vine-Snakes:** Slender, green reptiles that are indistinguishable from the flora until they move. They are non-venomous but can startle a climber into a fall.
- **Aether-Birds:** Tiny, feathered creatures with four wings that hum like dragonflies, darting between the spore-lamps.

## Navigation
- **Swinging Vines:** Natural grappling points that allow for rapid horizontal movement.
- **Dense Canopy:** In some areas, the foliage is so thick that the player must navigate entirely by sound and the glow of the spore-lamps.
