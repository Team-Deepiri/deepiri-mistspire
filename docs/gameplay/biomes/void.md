# Biome: Void

**Altitude Range:** 9000m - 12000m
**World Partition Layer:** `DL_PCG_Biome_Void`

## Overview
Between Crystal and Tundra, the Void is a thin-air crossing where sound dies and the sky turns a deep violet. Stars show by day. The climb continues above—Tundra through Pinnacle—so this band is a mid-ascent threshold, not the summit.

## Visual Description
The central spire becomes a needle of dark, polished stone, surrounded by floating "Void Shards"—islands of rock held in stable orbit by unknown forces. The stars are visible even during the day, appearing as cold, sharp points of light. The atmosphere is filled with a fine, glowing purple dust that reacts to the player's presence, swirling in their wake.

## Lore Fragment: The Thin Crossing
> "There is a peace up here that is terrifying. You can hear your own heart beating against your ribs, and it sounds like a drum in an empty hall. We aren't meant to be here. The air doesn't want us; the sky doesn't want us. And yet, I've never felt more alive than when I looked down and realized the clouds were just a carpet for my feet."
> — *The Nameless Ascendant*

## Environmental Hazards
- **VoidSickness** (code hazard for this band)

## Environmental Effects
- **Shard hops:** Floating islands force careful leaps and glider lines between stable rock.
- **The Gasp:** Thin air pressures stamina and oxygen systems harder than lower biomes.

## Visual Interest
- **Stellar Drift:** The background stars move slowly, giving the player a sense of the world's rotation.
- **Void-Flow:** Ethereal ribbons of purple energy that snake between the shards, acting as updrafts for the glider.
