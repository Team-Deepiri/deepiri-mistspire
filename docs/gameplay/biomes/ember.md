# Biome: Ember

**Altitude Range:** 5000m - 7000m
**World Partition Layer:** `DL_PCG_Biome_Ember`

## Overview
The Ember stratum is a violent, churning wound in the sky. Here, the internal heat of the spires bleeds into the atmosphere, creating a landscape of molten obsidian and glowing gas. It is a place of constant motion and thermal instability.

## Visual Description
The ground is a crust of black, glass-like obsidian, often fractured by rivers of sluggish, orange-white lava. Massive geothermal vents blast columns of superheated steam into the air, creating a permanent haze of sulfur and ash. The sky is a bruised purple-red, illuminated from below by the rhythmic pulse of the volcanic veins.

## Flora & Fauna
- **Cinder-Ferns:** Metallic-looking plants that feed on the sulfurous gas. Their leaves glow a dull red when they are saturated with heat.
- **Ash-Wraiths:** Wispy, heat-distorted entities that congregate around the vents. They are mostly harmless but can obscure a climber's path at critical moments.
- **Magma-Crabs:** Heavily armored crustaceans that bathe in the lava flows, their shells reinforced by the minerals found in the molten rock.

## Environmental Hazards
- **HeatExhaustion** (code hazard for this band)
- **Magmatic Bursts:** Small, unpredictable eruptions that can propel molten rock across the player's path.
- **Toxic Haze:** Low-lying areas collect heavy, unbreathable gases. Staying in these pockets too long will deplete the player's stamina rapidly.
