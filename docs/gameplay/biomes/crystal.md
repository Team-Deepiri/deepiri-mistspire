# Biome: Crystal

**Altitude Range:** 7000m - 9000m
**World Partition Layer:** `DL_PCG_Biome_Crystal`

## Overview
Above the volatile heat of the Ember reaches, the world turns cold and brittle. The Crystal biome is a cathedral of light, where the very rock has been transmuted into translucent, razor-sharp geometric structures. It is a place of absolute clarity and terrifying fragility.

## Visual Description
Light does not simply fall here; it is captured, refracted, and reborn. The spires are encrusted with massive quartz-like growths that pulse with a faint, internal rhythm. Prismatic "rainbow-bleeds" dance across the player’s vision as they move, created by the interplay of distant sun-stars and the refractive surfaces of the environment. The ground is a mosaic of shattered glass, crunching musically underfoot.

## Lore Fragment: The Singing Glass
> "Do not touch the blue clusters. They don't just cut the skin; they cut the soul. I spent an hour listening to a single spire hum in the wind. It wasn't a song—it was a calculation. The world up here isn't made of dirt; it's made of geometry."
> — *Kaelen, Master Cartographer*

## Flora & Fauna
- **Prism-Vines:** Non-organic, mineral-based growths that spread like frost over the crystalline faces. They pulse with light when touched.
- **Mirror-Moths:** Insects with wings made of thin mica-sheets. They are almost invisible until they catch the light, appearing as sudden flashes of silver in the dark corners of the caves.
- **Glass-Golems:** Rare, slow-moving constructs of animated crystal that serve as the silent guardians of the deeper clusters.

## Environmental Hazards
- **SharpShards** (code hazard for this band)
- **Resonant Fractures:** Certain areas are tuned to specific frequencies. A heavy jump or a loud sound can cause the surrounding floor to shatter instantly.
- **Light Blindness:** The intensity of the refracted light can temporarily blind players who do not navigate through the shadows of the larger formations.
