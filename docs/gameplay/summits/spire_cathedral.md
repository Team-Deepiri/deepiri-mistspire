# Summit: Spire Cathedral

**Altitude:** 5500m
**Marker ID:** `summit_spire_cathedral`

## Description
A hollowed-out spire that has been carved into a gargantuan place of worship. It sits at the lower edge of the Void, where the silence is most profound.

## Visuals
Soaring arches and intricate stone filigree create a space of immense scale. The central "nave" is open to the sky, allowing the violet light of the Void to pour in. Massive statues of the "First Ascendants" line the walls, their stone eyes fixed on the path upward.

## Significance
A place of awe and intimidation. The acoustic properties of the Cathedral amplify the player’s own breathing and movements, reinforcing the isolation of the high-altitude ascent.
