# Summit: Cloud Garden

**Altitude:** 2500m
**Marker ID:** `summit_cloud_garden`

## Description
A sanctuary in the sky. The Cloud Garden is a cluster of floating islands held aloft by the concentrated buoyancy of the Giant Spore-Lamps. It is the most vibrant and peaceful location in the mid-ascent.

## Visuals
Waterfall-like cascades of silver moss hang from the edges of the islands, drifting in the updrafts. Rare "Sky-Lilies" bloom here, their petals shifting through the colors of the rainbow. It is a place of soft light and gentle sounds.

## Significance
A major rest point for climbers. The air here is oxygen-rich thanks to the dense flora, allowing for rapid stamina recovery and a moment of reflection before the harshness of the upper levels.
