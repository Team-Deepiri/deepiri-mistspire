# Summit: Valley Gate

**Altitude:** 200m
**Marker ID:** `summit_valley_gate`

## Description
Valley Gate is the threshold of the journey. It is a massive, natural limestone archway that frames the first clear view of the spires rising above the Mist. For many, this is the last time they will feel the damp chill of the lowlands before the true ascent begins.

## Visuals
The arch is covered in ancient, weathered carvings depicting the constellations. A single, glowing blue brazier sits at its center, acting as the first official Summit Marker. When activated, it casts a beam of light straight up into the fog, signaling the player's intent to the world above.

## Lore Fragment: The Choice
> "Every step before the Gate is a walk. Every step after is an ascent. Once you pass through the arch, you stop being a dweller and start being a climber. The Mist will try to pull you back. Don't let it."
