# Summit: Mesa Crown

**Altitude:** 1200m
**Marker ID:** `summit_mesa_crown`

## Description
The Mesa Crown is a flat, wind-swept plateau that sits atop the tallest spire in the Arid biome. It is a place of stark beauty and punishing winds. From here, the vast red deserts of the 1000m stratum are visible in all their glory.

## Key Features
- **The Aeolian Pipes:** A series of hollow metallic tubes installed by ancient climbers. They catch the constant Sirocco winds, creating a mournful, harmonic hum that can be heard for miles.
- **The Lookout:** A stone outcrop that extends over the edge, offering a vertigo-inducing view of the dust runners below.

## Lore Fragment: The Wind's Toll
> "The pipes don't just make music; they tell the truth about the wind. If they scream, stay in the caves. If they whisper, you have an hour of peace. If they are silent, run—for the Great Storm is coming."
