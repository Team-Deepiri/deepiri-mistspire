# Summit: Obelisk Prime

**Altitude:** 4500m
**Marker ID:** `summit_obelisk_prime`

## Description
Obelisk Prime is a geometric anomaly—a perfectly smooth, four-sided spire of black obsidian that pierces the very heart of the Crystal biome. It is cold to the touch and seems to hum with a low-frequency vibration.

## Visuals
The Obelisk acts as a massive mirror for the surrounding crystals. At sunrise, it becomes a pillar of fire; at sunset, a shard of ice. The Summit Marker is embedded at the very tip, requiring a final, precarious climb up a surface with almost no handholds.

## Lore Fragment: The North Star
> "It doesn't matter where you are in the Crystal reaches; you can always find the Obelisk. It's the only thing that doesn't refract. It just is. Some say it’s a anchor for the world; I think it’s a warning."
