# Summit: Orbital Needle

**Altitude:** 8000m
**Marker ID:** `summit_orbital_needle`

## Description
The apex. The Orbital Needle is a structure so thin and tall it seems impossible. It extends from the highest point of the Void into the darkening sky, where the atmosphere finally gives way to the black of space.

## Visuals
The needle is made of a strange, pale metal that doesn't rust or age. As the player reaches the summit, the curve of the world becomes visible below, wrapped in its bands of mist, forest, and fire. The stars are massive, unblinking eyes watching the final triumph.

## Significance
The ultimate goal. Reaching the Needle is the end of the physical journey and the beginning of the legend.
