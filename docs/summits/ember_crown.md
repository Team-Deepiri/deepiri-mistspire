# Summit: Ember Crown

**Altitude:** 2000m
**Marker ID:** `summit_ember_crown`

## Description
The Ember Crown is the caldera of a dormant (but still radiating) thermal spire. It is a ring of jagged obsidian that glows with a constant, sub-surface fire.

## Visuals
The "Crown" is often shrouded in a thick, orange smog. At its center is the "Obsidian Throne," a natural formation that offers a 360-degree view of the geothermal vents below. The heat is palpable here, warping the air and making the distant peaks dance.

## Lore Fragment: The Heartbeat
> "You don't just see the Crown; you feel it. It thumps. Every few minutes, a pulse of heat comes up through the stone. It’s the world’s heart, and it’s still beating, even if the rest of it is dying."
