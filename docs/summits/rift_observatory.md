# Summit: Rift Observatory

**Altitude:** 3500m
**Marker ID:** `summit_rift_observatory`

## Description
Perched on the jagged boundary between the Ember and Crystal biomes, the Rift Observatory is a sprawling ruin of stone and brass. It was once a place of study for those who sought to understand the stars.

## Key Features
- **The Great Astrolabe:** A massive, rotating mechanism that still tracks the movements of the celestial bodies. Players can interact with it to change the time of day or reveal hidden paths.
- **Star-Charts:** Faded murals on the walls that hint at other "Mistspires" in distant lands.

## Lore Fragment: The Watchers
> "They weren't looking at the stars to find God. They were looking for a way out. The Observatory proves that once, we were more than just climbers. We were dreamers with the tools to match."
