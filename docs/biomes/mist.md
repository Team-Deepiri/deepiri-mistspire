# Biome: Mist

**Altitude Range:** 0m - 1000m
**World Partition Layer:** `DL_PCG_Biome_Mist`

## Overview
The foundations of the world are lost in the Mist. This is the starting point for every Ascendant—a realm of grey-on-grey where the sun is a myth and the ground is a memory. It is a place of muffled sounds and damp, cold air.

## Visual Description
The environment is dominated by massive, featureless stone pillars that disappear into the impenetrable fog both above and below. Visibility is rarely more than twenty meters. Glowing blue guide-beacons, left by previous explorers, provide the only sense of direction. The world feels small, enclosed, and ancient.

## Lore Fragment: The Veil
> "We don't know what's at the bottom. Some say it's the sea; others say it's a void. All we know is that the Mist provides, and the Mist hides. You start here because there is nowhere else to start. You look up because looking down is a death sentence for the mind."
> — *Elder Oryn*

## Gameplay & Training
- **The First Grip:** This biome introduces the player to the core climbing mechanics on textured, damp surfaces.
- **Beacon Pathing:** Players learn to navigate by following environmental cues rather than a clear horizon.

## Atmosphere
- **Muffled Echoes:** Every sound is dampened by the fog, creating an eerie, isolated feeling.
- **Drip-Stone:** The sound of water constantly dripping from the heights is the only percussion in this quiet world.
