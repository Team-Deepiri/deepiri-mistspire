# Biome: Arid

**Altitude Range:** 500m - 1500m
**World Partition Layer:** `DL_PCG_Biome_Arid`

## Overview
The Arid expanse is a realm of sun-bleached stone and eternal dust. Between the heights of 500 and 1500 meters, the moisture of the lower mists is stripped away, leaving behind a landscape of jagged mesas and wind-carved canyons. The air here tastes of iron and ancient heat.

## Visual Description
The horizon is dominated by monolithic, rust-colored plateaus that rise like the teeth of a sleeping beast. Vast plains of fine, crimson sand stretch between these spires, rippling under the relentless wind. During the "Glare" hours, the stone seems to vibrate with heat, casting long, stark shadows that provide the only refuge for the weary climber.

## Lore Fragment: The Red Memory
> "The stone here does not speak in whispers, but in cracks. It remembers a time before the mists, when the sky was blue and the water flowed down instead of up. Now, it only thirsts. We found a carving in the deep canyon—a fish with wings, or perhaps a cloud with scales. Even the rocks have forgotten which is which."
> — *Journal of Elara, First Ascendant*

## Flora & Fauna
- **Iron-Thorn Succulents:** Low-lying, obsidian-dark plants that cling to the vertical faces. Their needles are said to point toward the nearest water source, though few have lived long enough to prove it.
- **Dust Runners:** Six-legged, chitinous creatures that skim across the sand dunes. They move with a clicking sound that mimics the rattling of dry bones.
- **Vulture-Kites:** Scavengers with translucent wings that catch the thermal updrafts, circling the mesas in silent, patient loops.

## Environmental Hazards
- **Sirocco Storms:** Sudden, violent bursts of wind that carry abrasive sand. A Sirocco can strip the paint from a glider in minutes and reduce visibility to zero.
- **Heat Shimmer:** At peak altitude in this biome, the air becomes so distorted by heat that distances are impossible to judge, leading many to misstep during a crucial climb.
