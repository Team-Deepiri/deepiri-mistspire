# Moodboard: Atmospheric Progression

## Core Vision
Mistspire is a journey of increasing clarity and isolation. The progression from the bottom to the top should feel like a transition from a damp, crowded basement to the roof of the universe.

## Vertical Color Palette
- **0 - 1000m (Mist):** *The Somber Start.* Deep teals, slate greys, and charcoal. High fog density, low contrast.
- **1000 - 2500m (Forest/Arid):** *The Living Middle.* Emerald greens, rich terracotta, and sun-faded gold. High saturation, dappled lighting.
- **2500 - 5000m (Ember/Crystal):** *The Harsh Reality.* High-contrast blacks, glowing magmas, and blinding prismatic whites. Sharp shadows, specular highlights.
- **5000m+ (Void):** *The Final Frontier.* Deep indigos, neon purples, and the absolute black of space. Ethereal glows, starlit clarity.

## Visual Pillars
1. **Verticality:** Every shot should emphasize the scale of the spires. Use "god rays" and fog layers to define depth.
2. **Ancient Tech:** Incorporate weathered brass and glowing blue crystals to suggest a lost, advanced civilization.
3. **Fragility:** The higher the player goes, the more brittle and delicate the world should appear (from moss to stone to crystal to thin metal).
